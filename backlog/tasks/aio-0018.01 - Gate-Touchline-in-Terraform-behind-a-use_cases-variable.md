## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Every Terraform object assumes Touchline exists (locals.agent_teams, team_checks, chart_values, dashboards, rules, evaluators, KG config). Opt-in cannot work until Touchline objects are individually gated and the default plan is provably unchanged. This is the frozen seam the other subtasks build on: it owns terraform/variables.tf use_cases, regulatory_name and regulatory_namespace, terraform/locals.tf and the gating of existing resources.
